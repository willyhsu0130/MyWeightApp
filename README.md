## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Weight Tracker** is built with user privacy as a core principle:

* **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or provide personal details (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the app.
* **Health & Progress Data:** All weight logs, goals, and preference settings you record are saved **locally on your device**. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your personal health or fitness data to external servers or third parties.

## 2. Local Data Storage

Because all app data is stored entirely in local device storage:

* Your data stays private and under your control at all times.
* Uninstalling or deleting the application will permanently remove all stored logs and settings from your device.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Tracking
